Agent Sprint Demo

A public demo of two AI agents working together, round by round, where anyone can watch the work happen.

What this demo is

Muse is the lead coordinator. Gemini is the researcher. They work in rounds on one real job: improving the lobby display loop for Hotel Brendle in Robstown, Texas.

Each round, Gemini researches one new item for the loop. Muse checks every fact against real sources and corrects what is wrong — before anything is published. Then the loop gains one new verified addition.

This is a 2-hour sprint: 8 rounds, one every 15 minutes, then it stops. After the sprint, the loop goes back to a weekly rhythm, because lobby content does not naturally change every 15 minutes. The fast pace is how you watch the machine work, not a forever speed.

One rule for every round: no fake changes. If a round cannot verify something, it says so instead of inventing an update.

Sprint status: Not started. Rounds will appear below when the sprint begins.

The team

  • Roy — owner. Sets the goals, makes the decisions, and gives the final yes on anything consequential.
  • Muse — lead coordinator. Breaks goals into tasks, hands them out, checks the work, and reports back once.
  • Copilot — planning and quality checking.
  • Gemini — research and verification.
  • ChatGPT — strategy, drafting, and analysis.
  • Grok — stress-testing.

Full roster, code words, and working rules live in the context/ folder of this repo.

Approval gates

Nothing consequential moves without Roy's explicit yes on that specific item — no spending, sending, posting, publishing, filing, provisioning, deleting, sharing publicly, changing an account, or signing. Silence is never a yes. Items that time out are held, not approved. Muse may approve internal, reversible steps only, and every such approval is logged so Roy can review or reverse it.

The lobby loop

The Hotel Brendle lobby loop is a rotating display for the hotel lobby — general information and videos only. It currently has 11 slides:

  • Welcome message — "Welcome in, neighbors"
  • Community Board
  • Being Good Neighbors
  • Landmark slide — the hotel's history, designated 1984, Marker No. 2571
  • Around Robstown — including Church of Grace (Wednesday Bible study, Sunday worship) and Route 27 / Stop 1266
  • Two reserved slots for the "Fixing the Brendle" video series
  • Sign-off — "Be careful kids, have fun."

Guardrail: the loop never shows tenant names, rent amounts, or any private data. It is general information only.
