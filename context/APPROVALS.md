# Approvals

The approval gates, in plain words. Adopted by Roy 2026-10-25. Seeded from the Approval Matrix Policy in the shared record.

## The standing gate

Nothing spends, sends, posts, publishes, files externally, provisions, deletes, shares publicly, changes an account, or signs anything without Roy's explicit yes on that specific item.

Muse must not approve for Roy. Silence is never a yes for a consequential action.

## Action kind comes before priority

- **Consequential** — any action in the standing gate above.
- **Internal / Reversible** — a step that stays inside the system, never spends, and can be undone or re-decided by Roy. Examples: moving a task card between stages, filing a verified note, marking a return verified, preparing a draft for Roy's review.

A consequential action is never reclassified as internal because it is old, low priority, or has waited a long time.

## Who decides

| Priority | Who decides | If there is no answer |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Roy only | No timeout. Escalates immediately. No Muse approval. |
| High | Roy only for consequential items | Held, not approved. |
| Medium | Muse may approve internal steps only, after 72 hours' notice | Internal step may proceed after 72 hours. Consequential item stays Held. |
| Low | Muse may approve internal steps only, after 24 hours' notice | Internal step may proceed after 24 hours. Consequential item stays Held. |

Timeout means **Held, not approved** — for every consequential item, at every priority. There is no setting that turns consequential auto-approval on.

## Logging

Every Medium or Low internal approval by Muse needs a full audit log entry: task ID, what was approved, why it qualified, and how to reverse it. Every such approval is reported in the next daily digest, so Roy can review or reverse it.

## The daily check-in

Roy gets one consolidated approval digest per day, bundling every pending Roy-only item with its task ID, what it would do, and its priority. Urgent items may still escalate immediately, outside the digest.

## What this means for this demo

Updating the sprint board and the lobby-loop preview is internal and reversible. Putting the loop on the actual lobby TV, contacting a vendor, buying anything, or posting anything publicly is consequential — it needs Roy's explicit yes on that specific item, and Dane's yes gates any install in his building.
